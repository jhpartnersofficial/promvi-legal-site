PROMVI Privacy Policy

Service: PROMVI Operator: 제이에이치파트너스 (English display name: JH Partners), a business registered in the Republic of Korea, which operates the PROMVI service Contact, including privacy questions: jhpartnersofficial@gmail.com Effective date: 2026-11-11

1. Summary

PROMVI is a video teleprompter for Android and iOS. It has no account or sign-in. It is free to use with ads and has an optional paid Pro subscription (monthly or yearly) that removes ads and the Free limits. Your scripts and videos are stored on your device. PROMVI runs no server for your scripts, videos or settings, and the app's own code does not upload them. PROMVI operates one small service used only for app-review access: it is contacted only if someone opens Review access and types a review code (see section 5). Android's backup and the Save and Share actions you choose can copy them elsewhere (see sections 2 and 4). The ads and the subscription are provided through services of other companies, which receive some technical information as described in section 5. PROMVI is intended for people aged 18 and over, or 19 and over if you live in the Republic of Korea.

2. What stays on your device

  • Scripts you write or paste, stored in the app's local database.
  • Videos you record, stored in the app's private storage until you delete them. A copy you save to your gallery is yours to manage; PROMVI does not delete it.
  • Settings, such as language and recording options.

This information is used on your device only to provide the app's features: writing scripts, recording, playing back, saving and sharing. Deleting the app removes the data held inside it (see Backups below).

What the app itself does

  • It has no account, sign-in or user profile, and no server that holds your scripts, videos or settings.
  • The app's own code makes no network requests of its own, except one: if you open Review access and type a review code, the app sends that code to PROMVI's review-access service (section 5), and later sends the session token it receives back to check that the access is still valid. It is never contacted before the age check has admitted you, and it is not used at all otherwise. The app includes no analytics or crash-reporting library of its own. Network requests are made by the advertising and subscription components of other companies described in section 5; those companies' components may use the information they receive for purposes such as analytics, as described there.
  • A video is copied into your gallery only when you tap Save to device, and leaves the app only through the system share sheet when you tap Share (you choose where it goes).
  • The only web addresses the app opens are links to this policy and to the terms of use, in your browser, and the page for managing subscriptions (on Android the Google Play page, on iOS the subscriptions page of your Apple Account), when you tap Manage subscription.
  • Libraries. The app includes libraries written by other companies (see section 5). PROMVI does not give them your scripts, videos or settings. PROMVI cannot describe everything those libraries do on every device; their behavior is governed by their own vendors and their own privacy policies.
  • Subscription status on the device. After a purchase or restore, the app keeps the subscription status it received on your device and re-checks it when the app opens or returns to the foreground. If the status cannot be re-checked, Pro continues until the end of the paid period shown in that status, but for no more than 35 days after the last successful check; after that the app returns to Free until the check succeeds. The status also records when it was last confirmed, so that a changed device clock does not extend Pro.
  • Backups. Android may back up app data, depending on your device, Android version, Google account, backup settings, storage quota and encryption. In this version, your scripts and app settings stay eligible for Android's system backup, which can copy them to your own Google account or move them to a new device when you set one up. PROMVI's backup rules tell Android to leave out the videos you record, both for cloud backup and for device-to-device transfer, so they should stay on the device unless you save or share them yourself; this is a setting in the app, and PROMVI has tested it with a local backup and restore on a test device, not end to end with Google's cloud or between two devices. PROMVI cannot promise that a backup is made, that it is current, or that anything can be restored. Videos you save to your gallery or share with another app are outside this setting and follow that app's or service's own rules. On iOS, device backups (iCloud or a computer) can include an app's data; PROMVI has not verified whether recorded videos are included in an iOS backup, so do not assume they are excluded.
  • Your device or app store may collect crash and usage reports under its own settings. PROMVI does not control that.

PROMVI shares nothing by itself. A video leaves the app only when you choose Share (the system share sheet; you pick the destination app and PROMVI cannot control what that app does with the file) or Save to device.

5. Third-party components in this version

  • Ads (Free plan). Free users see ads served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK): a banner at the bottom of the script list and an occasional full-screen ad when you return to the script list after saving a video. No ads are shown while you record, save or purchase, and Pro users see none. PROMVI asks for non-personalized ads only and does not ask for app-tracking permission. Where the law requires it, Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) is shown, and ads are not requested until it allows them; you can reopen your choices in Settings when that option is offered. According to Google's published data disclosure for the Google Mobile Ads SDK (the version in this app is 25.5.0), the SDK automatically collects and shares with Google: your IP address (which Google may use to estimate your general location), product interactions (such as app launches, taps and video views), diagnostic information (such as app launch time, hang rate and energy usage), advertising data (information about the ads shown), and device or account identifiers (on Android the advertising ID and an app set ID; on iOS an app-scoped identifier and, only if you allow tracking, the advertising identifier). Google lists the purposes as advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, and says the data is encrypted in transit. This is collection by the Google component, not by PROMVI's own code; PROMVI does not receive or store this data. See Google's own privacy information for what it does with it. In your Android settings you can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ads personalization. On iOS, after Google's consent form (where it is required) and only for adults on the Free plan, PROMVI shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before the advertising component starts. If you allow tracking, Google may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) for advertising and analytics; Google's own privacy declaration for its component treats its device identifier as data used for tracking. If you decline, no advertising identifier is provided to the app, ads are still shown and the app works the same. In both cases PROMVI requests non-personalized ads only, and your answer never turns personalized ads on; Google's SKAdNetwork identifier is declared in the app so that installs can be measured without identifying you.
  • When the advertising component starts. The Google advertising component is part of the app, and the operating system can load parts of it when the app process starts. PROMVI's own code asks the component to initialize, and requests ads, only after the age check has shown the age that applies to you (18, or 19 if you live in the Republic of Korea) and, where required, after Google's consent form allows it, and only for Free users. PROMVI has not been able to confirm whether the Google component sends any information before the age check, so this policy does not promise that it does not.
  • Subscriptions (Pro). Payment is handled by Google Play on Android and by Apple on iOS; PROMVI never receives your card details. RevenueCat, a subscription-management service, receives an anonymous app user identifier, your purchase history and subscription status, and technical information such as device type and operating system, and returns whether Pro is active. RevenueCat describes itself as a processor of this information on behalf of the apps that use it, and stores it on servers in the United States. PROMVI does not give RevenueCat your name, e-mail address or other contact details. RevenueCat's documentation describes the IP address and the advertising ID as optional attributes that an app sends only by calling a specific helper function; PROMVI's app does not call it and sends no such attributes. Whether RevenueCat's servers also log the IP address of an ordinary request is not stated in the documentation PROMVI could read, so PROMVI cannot describe that. PROMVI's code does not start the subscription service until the age check has been completed and allows the app, so the app itself makes no request to it for a person who has not yet passed the check or who is under the minimum age; PROMVI has not captured network traffic to confirm that the library sends nothing before then. PROMVI has no account for you, so your Google Play account is how a subscription is restored. You can manage or cancel a subscription in Google Play.
  • Review access (only if you enter a review code). Google Play and Apple App Review reviewers can be given a code that temporarily turns on Pro features for review; it is not a purchase and changes no subscription. When a code is typed, PROMVI's review-access service (run on Supabase, in its Seoul, South Korea region) receives the code and then a random session token. It processes: the code and token (kept only as keyed hashes, never in readable form), the time of each attempt and its result, and your device's network (IP) address, of which only a keyed hash is kept, for limiting repeated wrong attempts. No device identifier, script, video or purchase information is sent. The failure records used for limiting are deleted automatically after one day. Attempt and result records are deleted automatically once they are older than 90 days, by a job that runs once a day; codes and sessions end when they expire or are revoked, and their hashed records are removed by the same job 90 days after they ended. The hosting provider's own request logs may also record requests to the service for a short period under its terms (its published Free plan table lists one day for API logs); PROMVI has not verified exactly what those logs contain. This service is not contacted by people who do not enter a review code.
  • Other components. The build also includes Google Play services components that come with the Google Play Billing and advertising libraries. On iOS the build includes Apple system frameworks and the same advertising and subscription libraries.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting library is included.

6. What we do not do

  • No account, sign-in or profile.
  • No analytics or tracking of what you write or record, and no analytics library of PROMVI's own. (The Google advertising component processes some data for analytics, as described in section 5.)
  • No sale of your data by PROMVI.
  • No ads while you record, save or purchase, and none for Pro users.

8. Age

PROMVI is intended for people aged 18 and over, or 19 and over if you live in the Republic of Korea, and is not directed at children or teenagers. It is not a children's app.

  • Age check. When you first open the app it asks for the day, month and year you were born, before anything else. The app also asks whether you live in the Republic of Korea or in another country, because the age that applies differs (19 in the Republic of Korea, 18 elsewhere). It keeps only the result of the check (below or at/above that age) and which of the two country groups applied, not the date you entered; to avoid a wrong answer it also reads your device's region and time zone, on the device only and only as a hint (never your location), and if they point to the Republic of Korea the Korean age applies. If the result is below the age that applies, the app cannot be used, and the app keeps the time of that result. The details can be entered again only after 24 hours, so that a typing mistake can be corrected; until then the result cannot be changed from inside the app. Clearing the app's data or reinstalling starts the check again, and Android's backup may restore the stored result.
  • Ads. PROMVI's own code asks the advertising component to start, and requests ads, only after the check is done and shows the age that applies, and never for people below it (see section 5 about parts of Google's component that the operating system may load earlier). Ads are shown only to Free users, and Pro users see none. Ads are non-personalized.
  • Purchases. No purchase can be started before the check is done. Google Play can require a parent's approval for purchases if a parent has set that up (for example with Google Family Link); PROMVI does not control this. On iOS, Apple can do the same through Ask to Buy and Family Sharing.
  • If you are under the age of majority where you live, ask a parent or guardian before buying a subscription or sharing a video with others. Sharing happens only when you choose it.
  • If you believe someone below the age that applies is using PROMVI, contact jhpartnersofficial@gmail.com.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of personal information. PROMVI keeps no personal information about you on a server of its own, and you can access and delete the data on your device yourself. Subscription records held by RevenueCat are processed on PROMVI's behalf: contact jhpartnersofficial@gmail.com to access or delete them. For data held by Google (for example ad data or a device backup), use Google's tools. For any question, contact jhpartnersofficial@gmail.com.

10. International transfers

PROMVI does not send your scripts or videos to servers it operates, so it makes no international transfer of them itself. The review-access service (only used if you enter a review code) runs in the Seoul, South Korea region of Supabase; Supabase is the provider that operates it for PROMVI. RevenueCat stores subscription data on servers in the United States, and Google may process ad-related information, including your IP address, in other countries under its own terms (section 5).
