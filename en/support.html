PROMVI Support

PROMVI is a video teleprompter: write or paste a script, read it beside the camera lens while you record, then save or share the video.

Contact us

Email jhpartnersofficial@gmail.com. Please tell us your device and operating system, the app version (Settings, bottom of the screen) and what happened. We read every message and reply by email.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. PROMVI has no account or sign-in.

Where are my scripts and videos?

On your device only. PROMVI has no server for your scripts or videos. Delete a script from the script list menu, delete a video from Settings, Videos kept in the app, or delete the app to remove what is inside it. A copy you saved to your photo library stays there until you delete it. See the Privacy Policy for details.

What is the difference between Free and Pro?

Free lets you keep up to 3 scripts and record up to 5 minutes per video, and shows ads. Pro removes those limits and the ads. Pro is an optional monthly or yearly subscription. If Pro ends, your scripts and videos stay and you can still open, edit and delete them.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google, not by PROMVI, so they are managed there. On iPhone: Settings, your name, Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions. In the app you can also open Settings, Plan, Manage subscription. Refund requests go to Apple or Google Play under their own policies.

I bought Pro but it is not active. What can I do?

Open Settings, Plan, and use "Restore purchases" while connected to the internet with the same Apple or Google account. If it still does not work, email us with your device, the store you bought from and the order date.

How do I change my ad choices?

Free users can open Settings, Ad privacy choices, when that option is offered in your region. Pro has no ads.

Who can use PROMVI?

People aged 18 and over, or 19 and over in the Republic of Korea.

How do I ask for my data to be deleted?

Your scripts and videos are on your device, so you can delete them yourself. For subscription records held by our subscription service, email us and we will ask the service to delete them where the record can be identified. See section 7 of the Privacy Policy.
